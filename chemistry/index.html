How to use Nucleus to New York

  • Journey through chemistry: the Journey button at the top walks through the story of chemistry in order, from the first atoms after the Big Bang to PFAS limits in 2024. Each stop has an animation, the matching 3D scale, links and a check question. Use Next and Back (or the ← → keys).
  • Zoom through the scales of matter: scroll, pinch, or drag the gold knob on the ruler at the left to go from Earth and its atmosphere, through New York State, a city, a home and a lab bench, into a fizzing drop of soda, down to particles, a water molecule, an oxygen atom and its nucleus.
  • Drag the scene to rotate it. Tap the labels: each opens a standard or a lab.
  • The Here tab lists the standards that fit the scale you’re looking at. The Standards tab holds all 21 on the NYSED Chemistry course map, each with an activity, the full standard, its core ideas and the Reference Tables it uses.
  • Choose Regents or Honors at the top or with ⚙. Content marked Honors goes beyond what the Regents exam assesses. In Regents mode it is tucked behind a dashed “Honors extension” button you can still open.
  • Easier reading: the Aa Easier reading button (or ⚙ Settings) rewrites the Journey and every standard in shorter, simpler sentences, with key words, a Regents tip, tap-to-define help for underlined words, and 🔊 read-aloud.
  • Reference Tables: look for the yellow RT boxes. They name the tables in the 2025 Edition Reference Tables for Physical Science: Chemistry that each topic uses and link to the official NYSED PDF.
  • Practice: Regents-style clusters for every standard, and a map of every cluster from the released June and August 2026 Physical Science: Chemistry Regents exams, linked to the official PDFs and answer keys.
  • Labs: full-screen interactive labs for every unit, including simulated versions of the three required investigations (★ The Fast and the Fragrant, ★ Bend and Stretch and ★ Just a Drop).
  • Placemarks: on the globe and the New York map, tap a placemark (Syracuse, Massena, Indian Point, Hoosick Falls, the Finger Lakes and more) for its story and practice questions.
  • My results: the ✓ button on the Journey card makes a summary you can hand in.
  • Keyboard: + and − zoom, arrow keys rotate, Home returns to Earth.
