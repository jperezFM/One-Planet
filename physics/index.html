How to use Spark to Supernova

  • Journey through physics: the Journey button walks through the story of physics in order, from the Big Bang to the phone in your pocket. Each stop has an animation, links and a check question; the globe turns to where each discovery happened. Use Next and Back (or the ← → keys).
  • Zoom through the scales of the universe: scroll, pinch, or drag the gold knob on the ruler to go from a fusing nucleus and a hydrogen atom, through electrons in a wire, a camera sensor and a phone, the physics lab, an amusement park, Niagara Falls, New York, Earth and Moon, the solar system and nearby stars, out to the Milky Way and the observable universe. Sizes are real.
  • Drag the scene to rotate it. Tap the labels: each opens a standard or a lab.
  • The Here tab lists the standards that fit the scale you are looking at. The Standards tab holds all 23 on the NYSED Physics course map, each with an activity, the full standard, its core ideas and the Reference Tables it uses.
  • Choose Regents or Honors at the top or with ⚙. Content marked Honors goes beyond what the Regents exam assesses.
  • Easier reading: the Aa Easier reading button rewrites the Journey and every standard in shorter sentences, with key words, a Regents tip, tap-to-define words and 🔊 read-aloud.
  • Reference Tables: yellow RT boxes name the equations each topic uses in the 2025 Edition Reference Tables for Physical Science: Physics and link to the official NYSED PDF.
  • Practice: Regents-style clusters for every standard, and a map of every cluster from the released June 2026 Physical Science: Physics Regents exam, linked to the official PDF and answer key.
  • Labs: full-screen labs for every unit, including simulated versions of the three required investigations (★ Induction Junction, ★ Wheels to Watts and ★ Thermal Tales).
  • Placemarks: tap a placemark (Niagara Falls, Coney Island, Brookhaven, the first seat belt law, offshore wind and more) for its story and practice questions.
  • My results: the ✓ button on the Journey card makes a summary you can hand in.
  • Keyboard: + and − zoom, arrow keys rotate, Home returns to Earth.
