One Planet, Four Sciences

Earth and Space Sciences, Biology, Chemistry and Physics are not four separate subjects you finish and forget. They are four ways of looking at the same planet. The new NYSSLS Regents exams keep asking how Earth’s rock, water, air and life interact, and every science helps explain them.
